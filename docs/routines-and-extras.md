# Routines and everyday extras

## Routines

One phrase for several commands. On the **Routines** page, give a routine a name
("bedtime") and its steps, one per line, written as you'd say them:

```
set status to busy
switch to pajama bear
wait 5 seconds
open cozy cabin private
mochi sleep
```

"**Computer, bedtime**" (or "run bedtime") runs them in order. After an avatar
switch it waits a few seconds so the avatar is loaded before the next step. The
page checks each line as you type and shows what it will do (red if it isn't a
command). The tray menu and the [AI brain](ai-brain.md) can run routines too.

A routine's name only counts when the whole sentence is the name, so a routine
called "bedtime" never takes over "set status to bedtime".

## Undo

"**Undo**" (or "take that back", "change it back") puts back what your last
command changed: the avatar, status, outfit, toggle values, or chat mode. There's
an **Undo** button on Home and in the tray menu. It remembers the last 10 changes,
for 15 minutes.

## Do that again

"**Do that again**" repeats your last command.

## Timers and reminders

"Set a timer for 10 minutes", "remind me to drink water in 30 minutes": a chime,
the VR overlay, a tray notification, and your pet says it. "How long left",
"cancel my timers". Running timers show on Home.

## Dice and picks

"Roll a d20", "roll 2 d6", "flip a coin", "pick a number between 1 and 100",
"pick someone" (a random friend in your instance). The result goes to the
chatbox, so everyone sees it.

## Going home and back

- "**Take me home**": a new private instance of your VRChat home world.
- "**Rejoin**" / "go back": the instance you were in before.
- "Where have I been": your recent worlds.

These use [live updates](friends.md#live-updates).

## Safe mode

"**Computer, safe mode**" (or "panic", "get me out of here") is one phrase for
when something gets uncomfortable. Set it up in **Settings → VRChat → Safe mode**:
switch to a safe avatar, set your status to Busy, and go straight to a new private
instance of your home world. Its messages are only shown
to you.

## Breaks

**Settings → VRChat → Breaks** can remind you every 1, 2 or 3 hours to stretch and
drink water. "How long have I been in VR" any time.
