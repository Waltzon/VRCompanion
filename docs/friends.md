# Friends and live updates

## Friends

Any online friend works by their VRChat display name: "invite alex", "join alex",
"request invite from alex", "where's alex". For names that are hard to say, add a
nickname on the **Friends** page ("alex" means "xX_Alex_Xx"), or pick from
**online friends**.

"Who's online" and "where's alex" are only shown to you (Home, overlay), never in
the chatbox.

## Groups

On the **Friends** page, make a group ("the squad") with nicknames or VRChat names
separated by commas. "**Computer, invite the squad**" invites everyone in it who's
online and tells you who wasn't.

## Live updates

While the app runs and you're logged in, VRChat tells it what happens, and the app
tells you (sound, VR overlay, tray and Home):

- **"Alex is online"**: your VRChat favorite friends and friends with a nickname.
  All friends, or off: **Settings → VRChat → Live updates**.
- **"Sam is joining your instance"**.
- **Invites, invite requests and friend requests**: say "**accept**" or
  "**decline**" (the newest first), or use the buttons on Home.

Live updates also remember where you've been, for "rejoin" and "where have I
been", and power [worlds that set you up](avatars-and-worlds.md#worlds-that-set-you-up).

## Status

"Set status to busy / join me / ask me / online". **Status presets** (Settings →
VRChat) combine a color and status text under one name: "set status to
streaming". "Clear status message" removes the text. "Undo" puts back the status
you had.
